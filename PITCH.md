# 🔗 LithiTrust - Blockchain Traceability for Zimbabwe's Lithium Supply Chain

## The Opportunity: $18B Problem Waiting to Be Solved

Zimbabwe sits on **world-class lithium reserves** worth an estimated **$18 billion**, yet faces critical challenges that undermine its competitive advantage:

### Problems We're Solving

**1. Lack of Transparency**
- International buyers can't verify lithium authenticity or origin
- No immutable record of supply chain journey
- Fraud and counterfeiting risks cost buyers billions annually
- ❌ **Problem:** Buyers default to expensive verification audits

**2. ESG Compliance Burden**
- Miners struggle to document environmental & social practices
- International markets demand certified sustainable sourcing
- Manual documentation is inefficient and easily falsified
- ❌ **Problem:** Zimbabwe loses market share to competitors with ESG certifications

**3. Unfair Pricing**
- Miners receive commodity prices without premium for sustainability
- No incentive structure for responsible mining practices
- Water usage, carbon footprint, community agreements invisible to market
- ❌ **Problem:** Zimbabwe extracts wealth without rewarding stewardship

**4. Limited Access for Artisanal Miners**
- Small-scale miners excluded from formal supply chains
- Can't compete with large operations that have compliance infrastructure
- No platform to document and verify their batches
- ❌ **Problem:** Economic inequality in mining sector

---

## The Solution: LithiTrust

**A blockchain-backed traceability platform that transforms Zimbabwe's lithium supply chain through transparency, certification, and fair incentives.**

### How It Works

```
Miner Registers Batch
        ↓
Batch Gets Blockchain "Birth Certificate"
        ↓
ESG Data Documented (Environmental, Social, Governance)
        ↓
Supply Chain Tracked (Mining → Processing → Export)
        ↓
Buyer Scans QR Code to Verify Authenticity
        ↓
Green Premium Pricing (0-30% based on ESG score)
        ↓
Fair Revenue Distribution ✅
```

## Key Features Implemented

### 1. **Blockchain-Backed Batch Registration** ✅
- Every batch receives immutable blockchain hash (Polygon integration)
- Unique batch ID and comprehensive metadata
- 256-bit cryptographic verification
- **Impact:** Eliminates fraud, ensures authenticity

### 2. **ESG Compliance Framework** ✅
- Environmental: Water usage, carbon intensity, rehabilitation plans
- Social: Child labor checks, community agreements
- Governance: Safety practices, audit documentation
- **Impact:** Standardized sustainability metrics buyers trust

### 3. **Document Upload & Verification** ✅
- Upload certificates, audit reports, permits
- Verification status tracking (Pending/Verified)
- Complete audit trail for regulatory compliance
- **Impact:** International buyers get proof of claims

### 4. **Green Premium Pricing Calculator** ✅
- Base price: $12/kg lithium
- Green premium: 0-30% based on ESG score
- Real-time calculation for all batches
- **Example:** 1000kg batch with perfect ESG scores = +$3,600 revenue
- **Impact:** Financial incentive for sustainable practices

### 5. **QR Code Verification System** ✅
- Unique QR codes per batch linking to blockchain
- Buyers scan to verify origin and supply chain
- Complete traceability from mine to buyer
- Downloadable certificates for regulatory compliance
- **Impact:** Buyer confidence and premium prices

### 6. **Role-Based Supply Chain Dashboard** ✅
- Miners: Register batches, track ESG data
- Processors: Update batch stages, add events
- Exporters: Manage logistics and compliance
- Buyers: Verify authenticity, see ESG scores
- Regulators: Oversight dashboard with reporting
- **Impact:** Seamless multi-stakeholder coordination

## Technical Achievements

### Architecture
- **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Real-time updates
- **Backend:** Express.js, PostgreSQL, RESTful APIs
- **Blockchain:** Polygon Mumbai (Layer 2) with fallback protection
- **Authentication:** Replit Auth (OAuth)
- **Scalability:** Serverless-ready, horizontally scalable

### Security Features
- Immutable blockchain hashes (can't be tampered with)
- Session-based authentication with secure cookies
- Role-based access control
- Private key management for blockchain transactions
- Fallback system if blockchain unavailable (never breaks)

### Database
- 5 core tables: Users, Batches, Supply Chain Events, ESG Records, Documents
- Relational integrity with cascade deletes
- Indexed queries for performance
- Type-safe ORM (Drizzle)
